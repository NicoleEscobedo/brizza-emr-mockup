## What this form is

This form is your acknowledgment of Brizza Aesthetics' late-cancel and no-show fees, and related financial terms that are already set in the clinic's operating SOPs. It is a required intake acknowledgment. It is **not** treatment consent, clinical photography consent, or a marketing authorization.

## Appointments, reminders, and notice

The clinic sends appointment reminders 48 hours and 24 hours before a scheduled visit (SMS is the primary method, with your communication preferences respected). Reminders are a courtesy. You are responsible for knowing your appointment date and time, and for giving notice if you need to cancel or reschedule.

To cancel or reschedule without a late-cancel fee, you must give at least **24 hours'** notice before the appointment start time. Notice time is based on the clinic's timestamps. Cancellations made with at least 24 hours' notice are not charged the late-cancel fee.


## Late-cancel fee

If you cancel or reschedule with **less than 24 hours'** notice, a **$50 late-cancel fee** applies.

[confirm with Clinic Ops Lead: any exceptions to the $50 late-cancel fee. The SOPs state that exceptions are not yet sourced and must not be invented. No illness, weather, or other exception is listed in this form until Clinic Ops confirms it.]

## No-show fee

If you miss an appointment and do not cancel in advance (a no-show), a **$50 no-show fee** applies.

The no-show fee may be waived only by the **Clinic Manager** or the **Owner**. Other staff, including the Coordinator, Medical Assistant, NP Injector, and Aesthetician, may not waive this fee on their own.

## How these fees relate to treatment and payment

These fees are separate from charges for services you actually receive. Completing a service and collecting payment are tracked separately in the clinic's process. This acknowledgment does not set service prices, membership or package terms, tax, or refund rules for completed services.

## Clinic-initiated changes

If the clinic must cancel or reschedule your appointment, that is recorded as a clinic-initiated change. This form does not charge you a late-cancel or no-show fee for a clinic-initiated cancellation.
