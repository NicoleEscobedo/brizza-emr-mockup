## What this form is

This form is your informed consent for a dermal filler treatment at Brizza Aesthetics. Written approval of your treatment plan and estimated cost is a **separate** step. Signing this form does not replace that approval.

This form is **not** consent for dissolving filler. Dissolve is a separate appointment, performed only by the Owner/MD. It has its own form, the Filler Dissolve Consent.


## The procedure and who performs it

Dermal filler is a gel material injected under the skin to add or restore volume and to change or soften contours in selected areas. The official products in the current clinic catalog are **Juvederm** and **Volbella**. Your provider will tell you which product is planned for you, and the proposed syringe quantity. Other products may be used only if they are later added to the official catalog.

A licensed nurse practitioner in the **NP Injector** role performs this treatment and obtains your informed consent. In limited situations, the Owner/MD, Dr. Valentina A. Rossi, may perform treatment when necessary. ## Expected results

Results are temporary. They vary from person to person. Some people see added volume or a change in contour in the treated areas. Swelling can change how the area looks at first. How long results last is not the same for everyone and depends on the product, the area, and your body. Results are not permanent. **No result is guaranteed.** This treatment will not fix every concern.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** **Juvederm** family products often last about **6 to 12 months** in commonly treated areas [SAMPLE]. The official catalog names “Juvederm” only and does not specify a Juvederm formulation; some formulations can last longer. **Volbella** often lasts up to about **12 months** in the lips or fine-line areas it is commonly used for [SAMPLE]. Duration depends on the product, the area, and the person.

## Common risks

Common risks include, but are not limited to:

- Pain, redness, swelling, bruising, tenderness, or itching at the injection sites
- Lumps, bumps, or firmness
- Temporary unevenness or asymmetry
- Temporary numbness or a feeling of firmness
- Discoloration
- Results that are less than, or different from, what you hoped for
- The need for further treatment, or for dissolving, if that later becomes appropriate

## Rare but serious risks

Rare but serious risks include, but are not limited to:

- **Vascular occlusion** (filler blocking a blood vessel), which can reduce blood flow
- **Tissue necrosis** (death of skin or tissue) from blocked blood flow
- **Vision loss or blindness**, including in rare cases permanent vision loss, and stroke
- Infection or biofilm
- Allergic or inflammatory reaction, which can be serious
- Delayed swelling or nodules, including weeks or months later
- Scarring
- Movement of filler from the treated area
- Need for urgent treatment, including dissolving or emergency care

**If you have severe pain, a pale, blotchy, or dark color change in the skin, or any vision change during or after treatment, tell the clinic right away or seek emergency care.** Your written aftercare will include the clinic's approved guidance on when and how to get urgent help.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** Additional rare risks:

- A bluish tint if filler sits too close to the surface (Tyndall effect) [SAMPLE]
- Delayed lumps or inflammatory nodules, including granulomas [SAMPLE]
- Cold sore (herpes) reactivation, especially with treatment around the mouth [SAMPLE]

**Sample urgent-symptom list:** tell the clinic right away, or seek emergency care, if you have any of the following [SAMPLE]:

- Severe or worsening pain
- Pale, blotchy, lace-like, gray, or dark color change in the skin
- A cool or mottled area of skin
- Any vision change, vision loss, or weakness on one side
- Shortness of breath or signs of a severe allergic reaction

**Sample contact pathway:** during clinic hours, contact the clinic and ask for your treating provider. If you have any vision change or severe pain, call 911 or go to the emergency room right away. After-hours urgent concerns: call (212) 555-0199 [SAMPLE]. For an emergency, call 911.

## Who should not have this treatment

Check any that apply, or check “None of these”:

- ☐ I am pregnant, think I might be pregnant, or am trying to become pregnant
- ☐ I am breastfeeding
- ☐ I have an infection, rash, or open skin problem in an area to be treated
- ☐ I have had a serious allergy or anaphylaxis, or I am allergic to the planned product, lidocaine, or related ingredients
- ☐ I have a bleeding problem or I take a medicine that affects bleeding, and I have not discussed this with my provider [SAMPLE]
- ☐ I have a history of cold sores (herpes), and treatment near the mouth is being considered [SAMPLE]
- ☐ I have had filler before, including a permanent filler or a filler of an unknown type [SAMPLE]
- ☐ I have dental work planned soon, or I have a current autoimmune flare, and I have not discussed this with my provider [SAMPLE]
- ☐ None of these

## Alternatives

Alternatives include doing nothing, using skin-care or other non-injection options, choosing a different product, area, or amount if one is appropriate, or considering other procedures your provider discusses with you. You are not required to have this treatment.

## Pre-care acknowledgments

I understand that I should complete or update my medical history, medications, and allergies before treatment, and that I must tell my provider about any changes. I will follow any pre-treatment instructions the clinic gives me.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** Use these sample pre-care steps unless your provider gives you different written instructions:

- Arrive with a clean face if you can. Avoid makeup on the planned treatment areas, if you can.
- Tell your provider about all medicines and supplements, including blood thinners. Do not stop a prescribed medicine unless your prescribing clinician and your injector agree.
- If it is medically appropriate and your provider agrees, avoid extra alcohol and optional supplements that can increase bruising (for example, fish oil, high-dose vitamin E, or ginkgo) for several days before treatment. [SAMPLE]
- If you have dental work planned, tell your provider. Many providers prefer to avoid elective dental procedures for about **2 weeks** before and after filler, when possible. [SAMPLE]
- Tell your provider about prior filler, cold sores, allergies (including lidocaine), and any change in your health, including pregnancy or breastfeeding.

A provider-approved clinic instruction sheet, when one is issued, replaces this sample list.

## Post-care acknowledgments

I will receive verbal and written aftercare instructions that are specific to the treatment performed. I will receive a printed or electronic copy. I will follow those instructions. I know how to contact the clinic about concerning symptoms. Clinical questions go to my treating provider. Aftercare does not replace a clinical assessment or follow-up.

I understand the clinic may send a brief post-treatment check-in message, and that this message is separate from a follow-up visit and is not a substitute for clinical care. The clinic offers or schedules a routine follow-up appointment for every dermal filler patient. Timing is set by the NP Injector. I may contact the clinic about concerns at any time, without waiting for that visit.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** Use these sample aftercare steps unless the written sheet you receive says otherwise:

- Swelling and bruising are common and can last several days to about **2 weeks**. A cool compress may help if your provider says it is appropriate; do not put ice directly on the skin. [SAMPLE]
- Avoid makeup for about **12 to 24 hours** if the skin will allow it to stay clean. [SAMPLE]
- Avoid strenuous exercise, alcohol, saunas, and steam for about **24 to 48 hours**. [SAMPLE]
- Do not massage or put pressure on the area, and avoid facials, massage, or other facial procedures for about **2 weeks**, unless your provider gives different instructions. [SAMPLE]
- Sleep with your head slightly elevated the first night if you can. Avoid elective dental work for about **2 weeks** when possible. [SAMPLE]
- **If you have any vision change or severe pain, call 911 or go to the emergency room right away.** Also seek urgent help right away for pale, blotchy, gray, or dark skin color; a cool or mottled area; or signs of a severe allergic reaction. During clinic hours, contact the clinic and ask for your treating provider.

A provider-approved clinic instruction sheet, when one is issued, replaces this sample list.

After-hours urgent concerns: call (212) 555-0199 [SAMPLE]. For an emergency, call 911.

## Right to refuse or withdraw

I can refuse this treatment. I can change my mind and withdraw this consent at any time before or during treatment. If I withdraw after treatment has started, the provider will stop as soon as it is safe to do so. Withdrawing consent does not change care I have already received. It also does not cancel charges for services already provided, or late-cancel or no-show fees that already apply under the clinic's financial policy.
