## What this form is

This form is your informed consent for microneedling at Brizza Aesthetics. Written approval of your treatment plan and estimated cost is a **separate** step. Signing this form does not replace that approval.


## The procedure and who performs it

Microneedling uses a device with fine needles to create tiny channels in the skin. The goal is to support skin renewal and improve the look of concerns such as texture or scars, depending on the plan your provider discusses with you. The official appointment type is **Microneedling**. The clinic SOPs do not name an official device, settings, depth, or number of passes (SOP 04 §5 and §8).

A licensed **Aesthetician** performs this treatment and obtains your informed consent as the treating provider for this service. A Medical Assistant may help with support tasks and may present or witness this form, but the MA does not obtain your informed consent and does not perform the microneedling.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** Sample device for this fictional project: **SkinPen Precision**, a commonly used FDA-cleared microneedling pen. ## Expected results

Results vary from person to person and often appear gradually. Some people see an improvement in skin texture or the look of treated concerns after healing. Redness and sensitivity are common for a period after treatment. More than one session may be recommended. Results are not permanent. **No result is guaranteed.** This treatment will not fix every concern.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** Redness and sensitivity commonly last about **24 to 72 hours**, with residual dryness or pinkness for a few days [SAMPLE]. A series of **3 to 6 sessions**, often about **4 to 6 weeks** apart, is commonly recommended [SAMPLE]. Improvement often appears gradually over the following weeks as the skin renews.

## Common risks

Common risks include, but are not limited to:

- Redness, swelling, warmth, tightness, or pinpoint bleeding
- Dryness, flaking, or temporary roughness
- Bruising or tenderness
- Temporary darkening or lightening of the skin
- Sensitivity to the sun
- Breakouts
- Results that are less than, or different from, what you hoped for

## Rare but serious risks

Rare but serious risks include, but are not limited to:

- Scarring
- Lasting **hyperpigmentation** or hypopigmentation (darker or lighter patches)
- Infection
- **Cold sore (herpes) reactivation**
- Allergic reaction to products used with the treatment
- Prolonged redness or inflammation
- Infection related to a break in the skin, including uncommon blood-borne infection risk [SAMPLE]
- Worsening of acne or an inflammatory skin condition [SAMPLE]
- Allergic or irritant reaction to a topical anesthetic, if one is used [SAMPLE]
- Tracking marks or scarring from uneven depth or technique [SAMPLE]

The combined list above is a sample final rare-risk list for this fictional project, pending Medical Director review. Risks can vary with device, depth, and products used with the treatment. [SAMPLE]

## Who should not have this treatment

Tell your provider if any of the following apply to you. Your provider reviews your history and decides whether treatment can proceed. This list is not a complete medical evaluation. Additional screening items below are sample items for this fictional project. [SAMPLE]

Check any that apply, or check “None of these”:

- ☐ I am pregnant, think I might be pregnant, or am trying to become pregnant
- ☐ I am breastfeeding
- ☐ I have an active infection, cold sore, open wound, or rash in an area to be treated
- ☐ I am using or have used isotretinoin (Accutane or a similar medicine) in the past **6 months** [SAMPLE]
- ☐ I have a history of abnormal scarring (including keloids) or of lasting pigment change after injury or procedures
- ☐ I have a bleeding problem or I take a medicine that affects bleeding, and I have not discussed this with my provider
- ☐ I have a history of cold sores (herpes) in or near an area to be treated [SAMPLE]
- ☐ I have active cystic or inflammatory acne, or a flare of eczema, psoriasis, or dermatitis, in an area to be treated [SAMPLE]
- ☐ I have a weakened immune system or a poorly controlled medical condition (for example, poorly controlled diabetes) [SAMPLE]
- ☐ I had a neuromodulator or filler treatment recently and have not discussed the timing with my provider [SAMPLE]
- ☐ None of these

## Alternatives

Alternatives include doing nothing, using a home skin-care plan only, or considering other clinic services your provider discusses with you, such as a chemical peel or a customized facial if those are appropriate. You are not required to have this treatment.

## Pre-care acknowledgments

I understand that I should complete or update my medical history, medications, allergies, and current skin-care routine before treatment, and that I must tell my provider about any changes. I will follow any pre-treatment instructions the clinic gives me.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** Use these sample pre-care steps unless your provider gives you different written instructions:

- Arrive with clean skin. Avoid makeup on the area to be treated, if you can.
- For about **5 to 7 days** before treatment, pause retinoids, AHAs/BHAs, benzoyl peroxide, scrubs, and other strong exfoliants unless your provider says otherwise. [SAMPLE]
- Avoid sunburn, tanning beds, waxing, and other facial procedures in the treatment area for the period your provider gives you.
- If you have a history of cold sores, tell your provider. An antiviral medicine may be recommended.
- Do not use isotretinoin within **6 months** before microneedling unless your provider specifically clears you. [SAMPLE]
- Tell your provider about all medicines, including blood thinners, and about any recent neuromodulator or filler treatment. Do not stop a prescribed medicine unless your prescribing clinician and your provider agree.
- Tell your provider about all allergies and any change in your health, including pregnancy or breastfeeding.

A provider-approved clinic instruction sheet, when one is issued, replaces this sample list.

## Post-care acknowledgments

I will receive verbal and written aftercare instructions that are specific to the treatment performed. I will receive a printed or electronic copy. I will follow those instructions. I know how to contact the clinic with questions or concerns. Clinical questions go to my treating provider (the Aesthetician). I understand the clinic sends a post-treatment check-in message after this service, and that this message is separate from any formal follow-up visit. A follow-up visit is scheduled only if my provider recommends one.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** Use these sample aftercare steps unless the written sheet for the treatment you received says otherwise:

- Keep the area clean. Use only products your provider approves. Do not pick, scratch, or exfoliate the skin while it is healing.
- Avoid makeup for about **24 hours**, if the skin will allow it to stay clean. [SAMPLE]
- Avoid strenuous exercise, sweating, swimming, saunas, and steam for about **24 to 48 hours**. [SAMPLE]
- Use a broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher) every day once your provider says the skin can have sunscreen. Avoid direct sun and tanning beds while the skin is pink or sensitive.
- Do not restart retinoids, acids, or exfoliants until your provider says the skin has healed, often about **5 to 7 days**. [SAMPLE]
- Call the clinic if you have increasing pain, spreading redness, pus, a fever, blistering, or redness that is getting worse after the first couple of days. For trouble breathing or a severe allergic reaction, seek emergency care.

A provider-approved clinic instruction sheet, when one is issued, replaces this sample list.

## Right to refuse or withdraw

I can refuse this treatment. I can change my mind and withdraw this consent at any time before or during treatment. If I withdraw after treatment has started, the provider will stop as soon as it is safe to do so. Withdrawing consent does not change care I have already received. It also does not cancel charges for services already provided, or late-cancel or no-show fees that already apply under the clinic's financial policy.
