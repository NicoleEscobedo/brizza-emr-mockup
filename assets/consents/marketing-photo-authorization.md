## This authorization is voluntary

This form is a **voluntary authorization** to use certain photographs of you for marketing or promotion. It is **not** a condition of treatment. You can receive care at Brizza Aesthetics without signing this form.

This form is **separate** from:

- Consent for treatment
- Consent for clinical photography
- Permission for appointment-related messages (which is also separate from marketing communication consent)

One permission does not authorize the others. Clinical photographs in your medical record are **not** approved for marketing unless you complete this authorization for the photos and uses described below.

## Right to revoke

I may revoke this authorization at any time by giving the clinic a written notice of revocation.

[confirm with Clinic Ops Lead and attorney: the address, email, or other method that counts as written notice]

Revocation is effective when the clinic receives it, except to the extent the clinic has already relied on this authorization. **Revocation does not undo material that has already been published or distributed.** The clinic will stop new uses under this form after revocation is received, but it may not be able to remove copies already posted, shared, or printed by others.

Revoking this authorization does not affect your treatment consent or your clinical photography consent. It does not affect your right to receive treatment.
