# Fonts (local copies, no CDN)

All fonts are copied from the box font library for offline use. No Google Fonts or other CDN requests are made at runtime.

These families are published under the SIL Open Font License (OFL), Version 1.1.

| Theme | Display | Text | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 Quiet Atelier (default) | Cormorant Garamond | Jost | `CormorantGaramond-Variable.ttf`, `CormorantGaramond-Italic-Variable.ttf`, `Jost-Variable.ttf`, `Jost-Italic-Variable.ttf` |
| Lapis Conservatory | Syne | IBM Plex Sans | `Syne-Variable.ttf`, `IBMPlexSans-Variable.ttf`, `IBMPlexSans-Italic-Variable.ttf` |

Theme 03 Architectural Classic v2 (Marcellus, Archivo) was removed in Round 3; those files are kept in `_superseded/theme-03/`.

Source paths on the box (read-only copies): `/usr/share/fonts/truetype/sand-box/google/`.
