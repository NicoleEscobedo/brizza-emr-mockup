## What this form is

This form is your consent for **clinical photographs** only. Clinical photographs support treatment planning and documentation. They become part of your medical record.

This form does **not** allow the clinic to use your photographs for marketing or promotion. Marketing use requires a separate marketing photo authorization. Consent for treatment, consent for clinical photography, and marketing photo authorization are separate. One does not authorize the others.

Clinical photographs are **optional**. You may decline. Declining clinical photographs will be documented. Treatment is not automatically refused only because you decline photographs, but your provider may explain if missing photos would limit planning or documentation for a specific service.


## What may be photographed

If you agree, staff may take photographs (and, if needed, related still images from a visit) of the areas being evaluated or treated. Photos may be taken before, during, and after a service so your provider can compare change over time. Before-and-after photographs used for your clinical record are still clinical photographs under this form. They are **not** approved for marketing unless you sign the separate marketing authorization.

## How clinical photographs are used and stored

Clinical photographs may be used for:

- Treatment planning and comparison
- Documentation in your medical record
- Communication among authorized clinic clinicians involved in your care

They are stored in the clinic's EMR, in the Photos section of your chart, and may also be attached to the relevant visit or procedure note. Access is role-based. Staff may see only what they need for their job. The Marketing Specialist may **not** use these photos for marketing, and may access marketing photos only when a separate authorization is on file for that use.

Each photo record should include the photo date, category, related appointment or treatment when applicable, the staff member who uploaded it, and a link to this consent.

## Who may take and handle the photographs

Authorized clinical or support staff may take or upload clinical photographs for the uses above. Your treating provider remains responsible for clinical decisions that rely on those photographs.

## Alternatives

You may decline clinical photography, or you may limit the areas that are photographed, as noted above. You may also withdraw this consent later, as described below.

## Right to withdraw

You may withdraw this consent at any time by telling the clinic in writing. Withdrawal does not erase photographs already taken and stored in your medical record. Those record entries are kept under the clinic's record-retention rules. After withdrawal, the clinic will not take new clinical photographs under this consent. Withdrawal of this consent does not withdraw your treatment consent or any marketing authorization, which are separate.
