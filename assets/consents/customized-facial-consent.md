## What this form is

This form is your informed consent for a customized facial service at Brizza Aesthetics. “Customized Facial” is the umbrella service line. The official booking type is **Facial**, with one of these reasons: **Mini Facial**, **Facial**, or **Hydrafacial**. Written approval of your planned service, any included enhancements, and estimated cost is a **separate** step. Signing this form does not replace that approval.


## The procedure and who performs it

A customized facial is a professional skin treatment matched to your skin and goals. It may include cleansing, exfoliation, extractions when appropriate, a mask, moisturizer, and other steps or products your provider selects. A **Hydrafacial**, when that is the planned service, uses that official service method.

The clinic sources do not list approved enhancements or a step-by-step protocol. Any enhancements included in your plan will be reviewed with you before treatment.

A licensed **Aesthetician** performs this service and obtains your informed consent as the treating provider. A Medical Assistant may help with support tasks and may present or witness this form, but the MA does not obtain your informed consent and does not perform the facial.

## Expected results

Results vary from person to person and are often temporary. Some people see cleaner, smoother, or more refreshed-looking skin for a period of time. This is not a surgical or injection treatment, and it will not produce the same kind of change as those treatments. **No result is guaranteed.** This service will not fix every concern.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).**

- **Mini Facial** (30 minutes): a shorter or more limited refresh, consistent with the draft booking guidance in SOP 05 §3. Little to no downtime is typical. A temporary cleaner or brighter look may last a few days. [SAMPLE]
- **Facial** (60 minutes): a standard customized facial that may include deeper cleansing, exfoliation, and extractions when appropriate. Mild redness can last several hours. Results are temporary. [SAMPLE]
- **Hydrafacial** (75 minutes): a device-assisted cleanse, exfoliation, extraction, and hydration. Little to no downtime is typical. An immediate glow often lasts about **5 to 7 days**. [SAMPLE]


## Common risks

Common risks include, but are not limited to:

- Redness, warmth, tightness, or dryness
- Temporary sensitivity
- Breakouts, including after extractions
- Irritation from a product used during the service
- Results that are less than, or different from, what you hoped for

## Rare but serious risks

Rare but serious risks include, but are not limited to:

- Allergic reaction to a product
- Infection
- Prolonged irritation
- Pigment change
- Cold sore (herpes) reactivation, especially if extractions or a more stimulating step is used
- Scarring, which is uncommon but can occur, including after extractions or a reaction
- Eye irritation from a product or device [SAMPLE]
- Temporary increased sensitivity after exfoliation, extractions, or a Hydrafacial [SAMPLE]
- Breakouts after an occlusive product or after an enhancement [SAMPLE]

The combined list above is a sample final risk list for this fictional project and applies across Mini Facial, Facial, and Hydrafacial, including any sample enhancements used. Pending Medical Director review. [SAMPLE]

## Who should not have this treatment

Tell your provider if any of the following apply to you. Your provider reviews your history and decides whether the service can proceed. This list is not a complete medical evaluation. Additional screening items below are sample items for this fictional project and may depend on the facial type or enhancements. [SAMPLE]

Check any that apply, or check “None of these”:

- ☐ I am pregnant, think I might be pregnant, or am trying to become pregnant
- ☐ I am breastfeeding
- ☐ I have an active infection, cold sore, open wound, or rash in an area to be treated
- ☐ I am using or have used isotretinoin (Accutane or a similar medicine) in the past **6 months** [SAMPLE]
- ☐ I have a known allergy to a skin-care ingredient that may be used
- ☐ I have had a recent sunburn, used a tanning bed, or had waxing, a peel, laser, or another facial procedure in an area to be treated [SAMPLE]
- ☐ I have a history of cold sores (herpes) in or near an area to be treated [SAMPLE]
- ☐ I have a current flare of rosacea, eczema, psoriasis, or dermatitis in an area to be treated [SAMPLE]
- ☐ I had a neuromodulator or filler treatment recently and have not discussed the timing with my provider [SAMPLE]
- ☐ None of these

## Alternatives

Alternatives include doing nothing, using a home skin-care plan only, choosing a different official facial type if one is appropriate, or considering other clinic services your provider discusses with you. You are not required to have this service.

## Pre-care acknowledgments

I understand that I should complete or update my medical history, medications, allergies, and current skin-care routine before the service, and that I must tell my provider about any changes. I will follow any pre-treatment instructions the clinic gives me.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** Use these sample pre-care steps unless your provider gives you different written instructions:

- Arrive with a clean face if you can. You may be asked to remove makeup before the service.
- For about **24 to 48 hours** before a more stimulating facial, peel-like enhancement, or Hydrafacial, pause retinoids, strong acids, and scrubs unless your provider says otherwise. [SAMPLE]
- Avoid sunburn, tanning beds, and waxing in the area to be treated.
- If you have a history of cold sores, tell your provider, especially if extractions or a stimulating step is planned.
- Tell your provider about isotretinoin use in the past **6 months**, all allergies, and any change in your health, including pregnancy or breastfeeding. [SAMPLE]

A provider-approved clinic instruction sheet, when one is issued, replaces this sample list.

## Post-care acknowledgments

When aftercare applies to the service performed, I will receive verbal and written instructions and a printed or electronic copy. I will follow those instructions. I know how to contact the clinic about concerns. Treatment-related questions go to the Aesthetician. I understand the clinic sends a brief post-treatment check-in message after this service, and that this message is not a substitute for professional assessment. A follow-up visit is scheduled only if my provider recommends one.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** Use these sample aftercare steps unless the written sheet for the facial you received says otherwise:

- Mini Facial, Facial, and Hydrafacial usually have little downtime. Mild redness or sensitivity can last several hours. [SAMPLE]
- Use a gentle cleanser and moisturizer. Avoid retinoids, strong acids, scrubs, and hot environments (sauna, steam, or intense exercise) for about **24 to 48 hours**, or longer if your skin is still irritated. [SAMPLE]
- Use a broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher) every day. Avoid picking after extractions.
- Call the clinic if you have increasing pain, spreading redness, pus, a fever, or a reaction that is getting worse. For trouble breathing or a severe allergic reaction, seek emergency care.

A provider-approved clinic instruction sheet, when one is issued, replaces this sample list.

## Right to refuse or withdraw

I can refuse this treatment. I can change my mind and withdraw this consent at any time before or during treatment. If I withdraw after treatment has started, the provider will stop as soon as it is safe to do so. Withdrawing consent does not change care I have already received. It also does not cancel charges for services already provided, or late-cancel or no-show fees that already apply under the clinic's financial policy.
