## What this form is

This form is your informed consent for a neuromodulator (neurotoxin) treatment at Brizza Aesthetics. Written approval of your treatment plan and estimated cost is a **separate** step. Signing this form does not replace that approval.


## The procedure and who performs it

A neuromodulator is a prescription medicine injected into selected muscles. It temporarily reduces muscle activity in the treated areas. This can soften lines that appear with movement.

The official products in the current clinic catalog are **Botox** and **Jeuveau**. Your provider will tell you which product is planned for you. Other products may be used only if they are later added to the official catalog.

A licensed nurse practitioner in the **NP Injector** role performs this treatment and obtains your informed consent. In limited situations, the Owner/MD, Dr. Valentina A. Rossi, may perform treatment when necessary. A Medical Assistant may help with support tasks and may present or witness this form, but the MA does not obtain your informed consent and does not perform this injection.

## Expected results

Results are temporary. They vary from person to person. Some people see a softening of movement and lines in the treated areas. How soon results appear, how strong they are, and how long they last are not the same for everyone. Results are not permanent. Repeat treatment may be discussed later if it is appropriate. **No result is guaranteed.** This treatment will not fix every line or every concern.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** Onset is often **2 to 5 days**, with a fuller effect by about **10 to 14 days** [SAMPLE]. The effect commonly lasts about **3 to 4 months** for both Botox and Jeuveau [SAMPLE]. A Toxin Touch-up, if offered, is usually considered after the full effect is visible, often around **2 weeks** [SAMPLE]. Individual results vary.

## Common risks

Common risks include, but are not limited to:

- Pain, stinging, redness, swelling, bruising, or tenderness at the injection sites
- Headache
- A feeling of tightness or heaviness
- Temporary unevenness or asymmetry
- Flu-like symptoms
- Temporary weakness of nearby muscles
- Results that are less than, or different from, what you hoped for

## Rare but serious risks

Rare but serious risks include, but are not limited to:

- Drooping of an eyelid or brow (ptosis)
- Crooked smile, dry eye, double or blurred vision, or trouble focusing
- Allergic reaction, which can be serious
- Infection
- Difficulty swallowing, speaking, or breathing
- Dry mouth [SAMPLE]
- Reduced response over time [SAMPLE]
- Neck weakness, if nearby muscles are affected [SAMPLE]

**FDA boxed warning (plain-language summary):** For this class of products, the toxin effect can, in rare cases, spread beyond the injection area. That spread can cause serious symptoms such as trouble swallowing, speaking, or breathing, and these symptoms can be life-threatening. Seek emergency care right away if you have trouble breathing or swallowing, or if you have weakness, vision changes, or other serious symptoms after treatment.

## Who should not have this treatment

Tell your provider if any of the following apply to you. Your provider reviews your history and decides whether treatment can proceed. This list is not a complete medical evaluation. Additional screening items below are sample items for this fictional project. [SAMPLE]

Check any that apply, or check “None of these”:

- ☐ I am pregnant, think I might be pregnant, or am trying to become pregnant
- ☐ I am breastfeeding
- ☐ I have an infection, rash, or open skin problem in an area to be treated
- ☐ I have had an allergic reaction to a botulinum toxin product or to an ingredient in the planned product, including human albumin [SAMPLE]
- ☐ I have a neuromuscular condition (for example, myasthenia gravis, Lambert-Eaton syndrome, or ALS)
- ☐ I have trouble swallowing, speaking, or breathing
- ☐ I am taking an aminoglycoside antibiotic or another medicine that can affect neuromuscular function [SAMPLE]
- ☐ I have a bleeding problem or I take a medicine that affects bleeding, and I have not discussed this with my provider [SAMPLE]
- ☐ None of these

## Alternatives

Alternatives include doing nothing, using skin-care or other non-injection options, choosing a different product or treatment plan if one is appropriate, or considering other procedures your provider discusses with you. You are not required to have this treatment.

## Pre-care acknowledgments

I understand that I should complete or update my medical history, medications, and allergies before treatment, and that I must tell my provider about any changes. I will follow any pre-treatment instructions the clinic gives me.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** Use these sample pre-care steps unless your provider gives you different written instructions:

- Arrive with a clean face if you can. Avoid makeup on the planned treatment areas, if you can.
- Tell your provider about all medicines and supplements, including blood thinners. Do not stop a prescribed medicine unless your prescribing clinician and your injector agree.
- If it is medically appropriate and your provider agrees, avoid extra alcohol and optional supplements that can increase bruising (for example, fish oil, high-dose vitamin E, or ginkgo) for several days before treatment. [SAMPLE]
- Tell your provider about any change in your health, including pregnancy, breastfeeding, infection in a planned area, or a new neurologic symptom.

A provider-approved clinic instruction sheet, when one is issued, replaces this sample list.

## Post-care acknowledgments

I will receive verbal and written aftercare instructions that are specific to the treatment performed. I will receive a printed or electronic copy. I will follow those instructions. I know I should contact the clinic with questions or concerns, and that clinical questions go to my treating provider. I understand the clinic may send a brief post-treatment check-in message, and that this message is not a substitute for clinical follow-up. A follow-up visit is scheduled only if my provider recommends one.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** Use these sample aftercare steps unless the written sheet you receive says otherwise:

- Stay upright for about **4 hours**. Avoid lying down or bending over for long periods during that time. [SAMPLE]
- Do not massage, rub, or put pressure on the treated areas (including tight hats or face-down treatments) for about **24 hours**, unless your provider says otherwise. [SAMPLE]
- Avoid strenuous exercise, saunas, steam, and alcohol for about **24 hours**. [SAMPLE]
- You may apply makeup gently later the same day if the skin is intact and your provider allows it.
- Call the clinic for unexpected drooping, uneven results, or vision changes. **Seek emergency care right away** if you have trouble breathing or swallowing, severe weakness, or signs of a serious allergic reaction.

A provider-approved clinic instruction sheet, when one is issued, replaces this sample list.

## Right to refuse or withdraw

I can refuse this treatment. I can change my mind and withdraw this consent at any time before or during treatment. If I withdraw after treatment has started, the provider will stop as soon as it is safe to do so. Withdrawing consent does not change care I have already received. It also does not cancel charges for services already provided, or late-cancel or no-show fees that already apply under the clinic's financial policy.
