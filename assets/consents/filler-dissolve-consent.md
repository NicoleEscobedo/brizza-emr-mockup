## What this form is

This form is your informed consent for dissolving hyaluronic acid filler with hyaluronidase at Brizza Aesthetics. It is separate from the Dermal Filler Treatment Consent. Written approval of a planned treatment plan and estimated cost, when there is time for that step, is also **separate** from this consent.

## The procedure and who performs it

Hyaluronidase is a medicine used to break down hyaluronic acid filler that was placed earlier. This appointment type is **Dissolve**. It is performed **only** by the Owner/MD, Dr. Valentina A. Rossi. The Owner/MD obtains your informed consent. A Medical Assistant may help with support tasks and may present or witness this form, but the MA does not obtain your informed consent and does not perform this treatment.

This form may be used for a planned dissolve or for an urgent dissolve if a vascular occlusion (blocked blood flow from filler) is suspected.

## Expected results

Results vary from person to person. The amount of filler that dissolves is not always predictable. **You may need more than one session.** The medicine **may also dissolve some of your own hyaluronic acid**, not only the filler. After treatment, **the contour can look flatter than it did before the filler was placed.** **No result is guaranteed.**

## Common and serious risks

Risks include, but are not limited to:

- Swelling
- Bruising
- Pain
- Uneven results
- Allergic reaction, including rare anaphylaxis (a severe, potentially life-threatening allergic reaction)
- Incomplete dissolving, or dissolving more volume than planned, including some of your own hyaluronic acid [SAMPLE]
- Infection [SAMPLE]
- Hives, itching, or a delayed allergic reaction [SAMPLE]
- This treatment will not dissolve filler that is not hyaluronic acid [SAMPLE]
- If this visit is for a suspected vascular occlusion, the blockage itself can still cause tissue injury, scarring, or vision problems, and more than one treatment may be needed [SAMPLE]

## Who should not have this treatment, and allergy screening

Tell the Owner/MD if any of the following apply to you. The Owner/MD reviews your history and decides whether treatment can proceed, including in an urgent situation. This list is not a complete medical evaluation.

Check any that apply, or check “None of these”:

- ☐ I have a known allergy to bee or wasp stings
- ☐ I have had an allergic reaction to hyaluronidase or to a dissolve treatment
- ☐ I am pregnant, think I might be pregnant, or am breastfeeding [SAMPLE]
- ☐ I have an infection, rash, or open skin problem in an area to be treated [SAMPLE]
- ☐ I have a serious allergy, including to an ingredient in the planned dissolve medicine [SAMPLE]
- ☐ The filler to be treated may not be hyaluronic acid, or the product is unknown [SAMPLE]
- ☐ None of these

The Owner/MD decides, based on professional judgment, whether a skin test is done before treatment.

## Alternatives

Alternatives include waiting, massage if your provider says that is appropriate, and no treatment (doing nothing). You are not required to have this treatment. In an urgent dissolve for a suspected vascular occlusion, the Owner/MD will also explain why waiting or doing nothing may carry other risks.

## Post-care acknowledgments

I will receive verbal and written aftercare instructions that are specific to the treatment performed. I will receive a printed or electronic copy. I will follow those instructions. I know I should contact the clinic with questions or concerns, and that clinical questions go to the Owner/MD. A follow-up visit is scheduled if the Owner/MD recommends one.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** Use these sample aftercare steps unless the written sheet you receive says otherwise:

- Swelling, bruising, and tenderness are common. The area may look flatter than it did before the original filler. More than one session may be needed. [SAMPLE]
- A cool compress may help if the Owner/MD says it is appropriate. Do not massage the area unless you are told to do so.
- Avoid strenuous exercise, alcohol, saunas, and steam for about **24 to 48 hours** after a planned dissolve, if the Owner/MD agrees. [SAMPLE]
- **Seek emergency care right away** for trouble breathing, swelling of the lips or tongue, widespread hives, or other signs of a severe allergic reaction.
- If this visit was for a suspected vascular occlusion, also seek urgent help right away for worsening pain, pale, blotchy, gray, or dark skin color, a cool area of skin, or any vision change, and keep any follow-up the Owner/MD schedules.
- During clinic hours, contact the clinic and ask for the Owner/MD. After hours, or immediately for those urgent symptoms, seek emergency care. A specific after-hours clinic phone number is not sourced in the SOPs and is not invented here.

A provider-approved clinic instruction sheet, when one is issued, replaces this sample list.

## Right to refuse or withdraw

I can refuse this treatment. I can change my mind and withdraw this consent at any time before or during treatment. If I withdraw after treatment has started, the provider will stop as soon as it is safe to do so. Withdrawing consent does not change care I have already received. It also does not cancel charges for services already provided, or late-cancel or no-show fees that already apply under the clinic's financial policy.
