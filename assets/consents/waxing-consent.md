## What this form is

This form is your informed consent for a waxing service at Brizza Aesthetics. Written approval of your planned service and estimated cost is a **separate** step. Signing this form does not replace that approval.

## The procedure and who performs it

Waxing removes hair from the root by applying wax to the skin and then removing it. The official appointment type is **Wax**. The official areas in the clinic catalog are **Eyebrows**, **Lips**, and **Face**. A licensed **Aesthetician** performs this service and obtains your informed consent as the treating provider. Consults and follow-ups for this service are also performed by the Aesthetician. A Medical Assistant may help with support tasks and may present or witness this form, but the MA does not obtain your informed consent and does not perform the waxing.

The clinic sources do not name a wax product or method.

## Expected results

Results vary from person to person and are temporary. Hair in the treated area is usually removed during the visit. Hair will grow back. How soon it returns, and how it looks when it returns, is not the same for everyone. **No result is guaranteed.** This service will not permanently stop hair growth.

## Common risks

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** Common risks include, but are not limited to [SAMPLE]:

- Redness, warmth, swelling, or tenderness
- Temporary bumps around hair follicles
- Discomfort or stinging during or after the service
- Hair that is not fully removed, or that grows back sooner than you hoped
- Skin dryness or irritation

## Rare but serious risks

Rare but serious risks include, but are not limited to:

- Burns
- Skin lifting or tearing
- Folliculitis or infection
- Ingrown hairs
- Changes in skin color (darker or lighter patches)

## Alternatives

Alternatives include doing nothing (leaving the hair as it is), or using another hair-removal method if one is appropriate, such as shaving, tweezing, or another option your provider discusses with you [SAMPLE]. You are not required to have this service.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** Use these sample pre-care steps unless your provider gives you different written instructions:

## Post-care acknowledgments

I will receive verbal and written aftercare instructions that are specific to the service performed. I will receive a printed or electronic copy. I will follow those instructions. I know how to contact the clinic with questions or concerns. Clinical questions go to my treating provider (the Aesthetician). A follow-up visit is scheduled only if my provider recommends one.

**Sample clinical content (not in the clinic SOPs; for this fictional project only).** Use these sample aftercare steps unless the written sheet you receive says otherwise:

- Redness and tenderness are common for a short time. Avoid touching, picking, or scratching the area. [SAMPLE]
- Avoid heat, sweat, and tight clothing on the area, and avoid makeup or other products on the area, for about **24 hours**, or as your provider directs. [SAMPLE]
- Use a cool compress if your provider says it is appropriate. Use a sunscreen on treated facial areas once the skin can tolerate it. [SAMPLE]
- Call the clinic if you have increasing pain, spreading redness, pus, a fever, blistering, skin that looks torn, or a wound that is not healing. For trouble breathing or a severe allergic reaction, seek emergency care.

## Clinical photographs

Clinical photographs are optional. They are used only for treatment planning and your medical record, and only if you sign a **separate** clinical photography consent. This treatment consent does **not** allow the clinic to use your photos for marketing. Marketing use requires a separate marketing photo authorization. One permission does not authorize the others.

## Right to refuse or withdraw

I can refuse this treatment. I can change my mind and withdraw this consent at any time before or during treatment. If I withdraw after treatment has started, the provider will stop as soon as it is safe to do so. Withdrawing consent does not change care I have already received. It also does not cancel charges for services already provided, or late-cancel or no-show fees that already apply under the clinic's financial policy.
